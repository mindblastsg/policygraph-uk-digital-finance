# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PolicyGraph welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint. Debate about policy, evidence, and design should remain specific, respectful, and open to correction.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Declare relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.
- Accept corrections, especially where policy status or provenance is at issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate misinformation, doxxing, sexualised attention, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

For moderation concerns that can be discussed publicly without exposing personal or sensitive information, open a [moderation issue](https://github.com/mindblastsg/policygraph-uk-digital-finance/issues/new). For confidential reports about content or behaviour on GitHub, use GitHub's [Report Abuse](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) process. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

This project’s conduct rules are informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
